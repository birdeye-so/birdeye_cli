### What is the Birdeye Data Referral Program?

The Birdeye Data Referral Program lets you earn commissions by referring new customers to Birdeye Data.

Once approved as a Referrer, you'll receive a unique referral link and referral code to share with your audience. When someone signs up and purchases an eligible paid subscription using your referral link or referral code, you'll earn commissions on qualifying subscription payments.

Depending on your Referrer tier, you can earn up to 5% commission on eligible subscription payments (including overage invoices) for up to 6 months for every successful referral.

The more successful referrals you make, the more you can earn.

<br />

### Who can join?

Anyone with a crypto-focused audience or professional network is welcome to apply, including:

- Content creators
- KOLs and influencers
- Community leaders
- Developers
- Founders
- Educators
- Newsletter writers
- Anyone who regularly recommends crypto products and services

Applications are reviewed individually by the Birdeye Data team.

<br />

### How do I join the Birdeye Data Referral Program?

1. <Anchor target="_blank" href="https://bds.birdeye.so/auth/sign-in">Create or sign in</Anchor> to your Birdeye Data account.
2. Go to the <Anchor target="_blank" href="https://birdeye.so/data-api/referral">Referral page</Anchor>.
3. Click "Become a Referrer".
4. Complete and submit [your application](https://bds.birdeye.so/user/referral/submissions).
5. Wait for your application to be reviewed.

If approved, your account will automatically be upgraded to a Referrer account.

<br />

### Do I need to have a Birdeye Data account to apply?

Yes. You'll need a registered Birdeye Data account before applying to the Referral Program.

Create your account here: [https://bds.birdeye.so/auth/sign-in](https://bds.birdeye.so/auth/sign-in)

Apply here: [https://birdeye.so/data-api/referral](https://birdeye.so/data-api/referral "https://birdeye.so/data-api/referral")

<br />

### How long does approval take?

Applications are reviewed manually by the Birdeye Data team.nReview times may vary depending on application volume. We'll notify you once a decision has been made. Approval is granted solely at Birdeye Data's discretion.

<br />

### What happens after I'm approved?

Once approved, you'll gain access to the Referral Dashboard, where you can:

- View your referral link and referral code
- Track referrals and conversions
- Monitor commissions
- View reward history
- Request payouts (when eligible)

Your referral link will be generated automatically after approval.

<br />

### Can I get a referral link if I’m not a Referrer?

No. Referral links are only available to approved Referrers. You'll need to [submit an application](https://bds.birdeye.so/user/referral/submissions) first. Once your application is approved, your referral link will be generated automatically.

<br />

### What are the benefits of becoming a Referrer?

Depending on your referral tier, you'll earn commissions on successful subscription payments (including overage invoices):

| Feature | **Elite** | **Professional** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Commission Rate** | **3%** | **5%** |
| **Commission Period** | First **4 months** of each referred customer's paid subscription | First **6 months** of each referred customer's paid subscription |
| **Eligible Commission** | Paid subscription payments \+ overage invoices | Paid subscription payments \+ overage invoices |
| **Customer Discount** | **15% off** eligible paid subscriptions | **20% off** eligible paid subscriptions |

<br />

### Am I automatically accepted?

No. All applications are reviewed individually, and acceptance into the Referral Program is at Birdeye Data's sole discretion. If your application is approved, we'll notify you and upgrade your account to Referrer status.

<br />

### Does my referral link give new users a discount?

Yes. New users who sign up using your referral link or apply your referral code will automatically receive **15% for Elite tier or 20% for Professional tier** off their paid subscription, depending on your Referrer tier.

<br />

### If a customer clicks multiple referral links, who gets the credit?

Birdeye Data uses a **last-click attribution** model. If a customer clicks multiple referral links before signing up, the most recent referral link clicked prior to registration will receive the referral credit.

<br />

### If a customer clicks multiple referral links, who gets the credit?

Birdeye Data uses a **last attribution wins** model.

The most recent valid referral attribution before checkout receives the referral credit. If a referral code is pre-filled from a referral link but the customer replaces it with another valid referral code during checkout, the manually entered referral code will take precedence.

### Can I refer myself?

No. Self-referrals are not permitted. You cannot use your own referral link or referral code to receive commissions on your own purchases. Any attempts to do so may result in referral rewards being withheld and removal from the Referral Program.
