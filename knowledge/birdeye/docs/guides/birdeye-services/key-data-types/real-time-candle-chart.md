Birdeye brings you cutting-edge Real-Time Candle Charts, a feature designed to revolutionize the user experience for wallets and decentralized applications (dApps). With the ability to stream OHLCV (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume) data directly to our clients through WebSockets or APIs, Birdeye provides comprehensive coverage of every on-chain token, ensuring your users have access to the most accurate and up-to-date market information.

# Key Features

* **Streaming OHLCV Data**: Benefit from real-time streaming of Open, High, Low, Close, and Volume data, enabling users to make timely and informed trading decisions.
* **Universal Token Coverage**: Birdeye's extensive data coverage includes every on-chain token, ensuring a comprehensive and inclusive market view.
* **Enhanced UX for Exchanges, Wallets and dApps**: Essential for exchanges and wallets, our Real-Time Candle Charts are a valuable resource for users engaging in technical analysis. Stay updated with the market's latest trends and empower your users with critical insights.
* **APIs and WebSockets Integration**: Choose your preferred method of integration – whether through APIs or WebSockets – for a flexible and seamless user experience.

* [OHLCV Pair Data API](../../../data-api/price-ohlcv/get-defi-ohlcv-pair): Access real-time OHLCV data for a specific trading pair.
* [OHLCV Base/Quote Data API](../../../data-api/price-ohlcv/get-defi-ohlcv-base-quote): Get real-time OHLCV data of any 2 tokens in one blockchain network.
