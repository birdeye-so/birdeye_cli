This is an instruction to use your Web3 wallet for a seamless and secure login experience on Birdeye.so website. 

# **IMPORTANT NOTICE:**

1. **You need a Birdeye account before linking your wallet** and use Web3 Log-in feature. [How to set up a Birdeye account.](https://docs.birdeye.so/docs/birdeye-account)
2. Please check carefully the website URLs to be **birdeye.so** before connecting your wallet. Birdeye **DOES NOT ask for your private key** in any circumstances.
3. After linking your wallets to your account, you can log in to your account by "Web3 Wallet Log-in" feature, without using your email/password.
4. You can link up to 5 wallets to your account.

# LINK YOUR WEB3 WALLETS

Follow these steps to log in using your Web3 wallet:

## :white_check_mark: **Step 1: Set Up Your Birdeye Account**

* Before logging in, you need to create a Birdeye account. If you haven't already, please follow the instructions [HERE](https://docs.birdeye.so/docs/birdeye-account) to setup.

## :white_check_mark: **Step 2: Access the Birdeye Profile Page**

* Log in to your account and go to your profile page: [https://birdeye.so/user/profile](https://birdeye.so/user/profile)

## :white_check_mark: **Step 3: Link Your Web3 Wallet**

* On the login page, look for the "**Wallets**" session. 
* Click to button "**Link Wallet**"

## :white_check_mark: **Step 4: Connect Your Web3 Wallet**

* Follow the instruction on the popup to link your wallet to your account.
* After clicking to connect your Web3 wallet, you'll be prompted to authorize your ownership of the wallet. 

## :white_check_mark: **Step 5: You can now log in to your account  using the wallets you linked.**

* Look for "**Web3 Log in**" on the top right menu on your desktop or mobile devices.
* Sign the transaction to prove your wallet ownership
* You have been successfully logged in using Web3

# **IMPORTANT SECURITY TIPS:**

Ensure that you are logging in on the official Birdeye website [birdeye.so](https://birdeye.so). Be cautious of phishing attempts.\
Keep your Web3 wallet secure. Do not share your wallet credentials with anyone.

If you encounter any issues, please reach out to our support team on [Discord](https://discord.gg/7GVRPNynuU) or [X (formerly Twitter)](https://twitter.com/birdeye_so).
