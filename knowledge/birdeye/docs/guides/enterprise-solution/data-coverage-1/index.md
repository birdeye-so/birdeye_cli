Our platform delivers extensive real-time and historical data across all supported DEX–Chain pairs. This includes granular price feeds, event tracking to capture market movements as they happen. We monitor liquidity pools for size, inflows, outflows, and compositional changes, giving a clear view of capital shifts in the market. Comprehensive trading volume metrics are available at daily, weekly, and monthly intervals, alongside token-level information such as contract addresses, market capitalization, creation dates, and circulating supply. By combining live blockchain data with historical trend analysis, we enable in-depth market research, strategy development, and performance monitoring for traders, analysts, and institutions.
