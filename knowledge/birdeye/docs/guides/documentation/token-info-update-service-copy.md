Welcome to the Token Info Update Services provided by Birdeye. This service allows projects to update their token information on the Birdeye website. Our team at Birdeye will manually verify this information to ensure its accuracy and relevance to the community.

> 🚧 YOU DO NOT HAVE TO DO THIS! MOST TOKENS GET THEIR INFO UPDATED AUTOMATICALLY
>
> Birdeye automatically gets token data from multiple reputable public sources such as Coingecko or Coinmarketcap. Please be patient if you already submitted info to those websites. Our service, however, helps you to update token info quickly and have additional info such as project description and social links.
>
> **If you still decide to pay for our service, and your token gets info updated automatically from public sources, we will NOT refund for this reason.**

***

## How It Works

**SUBMISSION**: Fill out the forms provided on our website.

**SELECTION**: Choose either the Fast Track or Regular Track option based on your preference and urgency.

* **Fast Track**: Verify within 2 business working days, cost $300.
* **Regular Track**: Verify within 7 business working days, cost $200. 

**PAYMENT**: please process payment and provide payment evidence as indicated on the form. **Only request with valid payment evidence will be put into verification queue.**

**VERIFICATION**: Our team will manually verify the submitted information within the specified timeframe based on your chosen track.

## :exclamation: Important Notice

1. When provides List of wallet addresses NOT in circulation, with labels.\
   Please enter the wallet addresses in the format: **"\[address], \[label],"**.\
   If there are more than one address, **please add a new line**. THERE **MUST BE A COMMA (,)** AT THE END OF EACH LINE.
2. Please use short and concise labels. Some examples of wallets not in circulation:

* Team Cold Wallet
* DAO Treasury
* Burn Wallet
* Multisig Treasury

4. Examples of **CORRECT ✔️** format **(\[address], \[label],)**:\
   61aq585V8cR2sZBeawJFt2NPqmN7zDi1sws4KLs5xHXV, Team Cold Wallet,\
   EXJHiMkj6NRFDfhWBMKccHNwdSpCT7tdvQeRf87yHm6T, Community Cold Wallet,\
   CbU4oSFCk8SVgW23NLvb5BwctvWcZZHfxRD6HudP8gAo, Team Hot Wallet,\
   11111111111111111111111111111111, Burn Wallet,
5. Examples of **INCORRECT ❌** format: (These will NOT be processed)
   * 61aq585V8cR2sZBeawJFt2NPqmN7zDi1sws4KLs5xHXV, Team Cold Wallet\
     -> Missing comma at the end
   * 61aq585V8cR2sZBeawJFt2NPqmN7zDi1sws4KLs5xHXV - Team Cold Wallet,\
     -> Wrong separation character, you have to use a comma (,) instead of hyphen (-)\
     Community Cold Wallet, EXJHiMkj6NRFDfhWBMKccHNwdSpCT7tdvQeRf87yHm6T,\
     -> Wrong order, wallet address must come first
   * 3.CbU4oSFCk8SVgW23NLvb5BwctvWcZZHfxRD6HudP8gAo, Team Hot Wallet,\
     -> There is no need to add numbering
   * FVhQ3...CBmiKfekf, Community Hot Wallet,\
     -> Invalid wallet address format

PLEASE CHECK THE ADDRESSES CAREFULLY, ADDRESSES WITH INVALID FORMATS ARE AUTOMATICALLY REJECTED.
