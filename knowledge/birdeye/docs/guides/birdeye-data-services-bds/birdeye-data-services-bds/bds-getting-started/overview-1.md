The Cost and Usage Overview section of the Birdeye Data Services Dashboard provides a centralized, real-time snapshot of your account’s billing activity and data usage. This interface is designed to help you easily monitor your compute unit (CU) consumption, track current charges, and manage credit balances—ensuring full visibility into your subscription performance and cost efficiency.

Whether you're on a Standard or Enterprise plan, this dashboard empowers you to stay informed and in control of your data usage and billing cycles at all times.

## 💳 Billing Overview

* Total Estimated Bill: Displays your current estimated charges. In this case, it shows $0.00, indicating no current costs incurred.
* Due Date: The next billing cycle date. Example: Jul 22, 2025.

## Cost Breakdown

* Package: Indicates the current subscription plan and cost ($0.00 here).
* Additional API Usages: Any overages or extra usage costs (also $0.00 currently).

## Credit Balance

* Available: Any remaining balance from prepayments or promotions.
* Estimated Refill Before: When your next credit top-up is expected—empty here, meaning no refill is scheduled.

## 📈 Data Usage (Compute Units)

* This Period API: The number of compute units (CUs) consumed so far. Currently 0.
* Usage Bar: Visual indicator showing progress toward the monthly CU limit (30,000 in this case).
* Legend: Green dot = CUs used month to date.
