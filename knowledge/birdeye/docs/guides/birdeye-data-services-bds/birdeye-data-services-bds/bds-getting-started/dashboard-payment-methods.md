The Payment Methods section allows users to securely manage the payment options linked to their BDS subscription. This is where you add, update, or remove cards or banking methods used for billing.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/695c30256a36920d930f27275396268c4a19bb2d9c2f00ae9319103cd02fa685-Payment_Method.png" />

## ➕ Adding a New Payment Method

* Click the “Add method” button in the center of the card.
* A secure pop-up form or redirect will prompt you to enter:
  * Credit or debit card number
  * Expiration date
  * CVV/security code
  * Billing address (optional depending on setup)
* Submit the form to securely link your payment method.

## 🔐 Secure Payments

* All payment data is processed through a secure, PCI-compliant provider (e.g., Stripe, Link).
* Your billing information is never stored directly in the dashboard’s frontend, ensuring security.
